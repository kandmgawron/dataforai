## Product Warranty Terms – Meridian Outdoor Co.

### Introduction 

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we stand behind the quality of our outdoor equipment and clothing. This warranty outlines the terms and conditions that apply to products purchased from any of our 12 stores located across Scotland and Northern England. Our aim is to ensure that you enjoy durable, reliable outdoor gear, backed by our commitment to customer satisfaction.

### Warranty Coverage 

**Duration:** 

- **Equipment:** This warranty covers your product for a period of **two years** from the date of purchase. 
- **Clothing:** This warranty covers your product for a period of **one year** from the date of purchase.

**What is Covered:** 

- **Manufacturing Defects:** We warrant that all products are free from defects in materials and workmanship, under normal use and conditions.
- **Functional Issues:** Coverage includes repair or replacement of parts that fail due to manufacturing defects.
- **Electronic Components:** For products incorporating electronic components, we cover defects in batteries, sensors, and other electronic parts under normal use.

**What is Not Covered:** 

- **Wear and Tear:** Normal wear and tear, including fading, pilling, or shrinkage of clothing materials, is not covered.
- **Misuse and Abuse:** Damage resulting from misuse, abuse, accidental damage, or failure to follow the manufacturer’s instructions is excluded.
- **Modifications:** Any alterations or modifications made to the product by anyone other than authorised Meridian Outdoor Co. technicians will void the warranty.
- **Non-Compliance:** Failure to maintain the product as recommended, including but not limited to, insufficient cleaning, improper storage, and exposure to extreme conditions beyond the product’s specifications.

### Claim Process 

To make a warranty claim, follow these steps:

1. **Gather Documentation:** Collect your receipt or proof of purchase showing the date and store location.
2. **Contact Customer Service:** Visit any of our stores or contact our customer service team via phone or email to initiate your claim. Provide details of the issue and any supporting evidence, such as photographs.
3. **Assessment:** Our team will assess your product to determine if the issue is covered under this warranty. If approved, we will either repair the product or, where repair is not feasible, provide a replacement of equivalent value.
4. **Repair or Replacement:** Repairs will be completed as swiftly as possible. In the case of a replacement, you will receive a product that is new or refurbished to like-new condition.

### Additional Support

- **Extended Warranties:** For extended warranty options, please enquire at any of our stores. We offer extended service plans that can extend coverage beyond the standard warranty period.
- **Care Instructions:** To maximise the lifespan of your product and maintain your warranty coverage, follow all care instructions provided with your product.
