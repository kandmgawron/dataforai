## FAQs: Sizing & Fit

Choosing the right size and fit for your outdoor gear is crucial for comfort, performance, and safety. At Meridian Outdoor Co., we understand that sizing can sometimes be confusing, especially with different brands and styles. Below, we’ve answered some of the most common questions to help you find the perfect fit for your outdoor adventures.

### **How do I determine the right size for outdoor clothing?**

Each brand has its own sizing chart, and it’s important to refer to the specific chart for the item you’re purchasing. Our in-store signage and product pages online include detailed sizing information. When measuring yourself, use a tape measure to get the following dimensions:

- **Chest:** Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your armpits.
- **Waist:** Measure around your natural waistline.
- **Hips:** Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
- **Length:** Measure from the highest point of the shoulder (neck) to the desired length of the garment.

If you’re between sizes, it’s usually best to size up for comfort, especially in layers for cold weather.

### **What should I look for in terms of fit for different types of outdoor clothing?**

- **Base Layers:** Should be snug but not tight, allowing for movement and breathability.
- **Mid Layers:** Designed to provide insulation. Choose a fit that allows you to layer comfortably underneath and over your base layer.
- **Outer Layers (Jackets & Vests):** Should offer protection from the elements while still allowing for a full range of motion. A relaxed fit is generally recommended to accommodate multiple layers beneath.
- **Pants:** Should fit comfortably at the waist and thigh, with enough room to move freely. Consider the activity; tighter fits are ideal for climbing, while looser fits are better for hiking in colder weather.
- **Accessories:** Hats, gloves, and scarves should complement your clothing layers without being too bulky.

### **Do you offer alterations?**

We understand that sometimes off-the-shelf sizes might not fit perfectly. While we don’t provide in-store alterations, we recommend visiting a local tailor for professional adjustments to ensure your gear fits perfectly.
