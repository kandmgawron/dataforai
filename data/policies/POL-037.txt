## Cookie Policy for Meridian Outdoor Co.

This Cookie Policy explains how Meridian Outdoor Co., a UK-based outdoor equipment and clothing retailer with 12 stores across Scotland and Northern England, uses cookies on its websites and online services. Our commitment is to ensure transparency and compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other relevant legislation.

### What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to enhance user experience by allowing websites to remember your preferences, login details, and other information.

### How we use cookies

When you visit the Meridian Outdoor Co. website, we may place cookies on your device. These cookies are used for the following purposes:

- **Essential cookies:** These are necessary for the operation of our website, such as maintaining your session to allow you to log in and access customer-specific features.
- **Performance cookies:** These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. They provide information on page views, traffic sources, and user behaviour, enabling us to improve site performance and user experience.
- **Functionality cookies:** These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make (such as your user name, language, or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personalised features. For example, they allow you to upload images for product reviews.
- **Targeting cookies or advertising cookies:** These cookies are used to deliver ads that are relevant and engaging to you. They are also used to limit the number of times you see an ad and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. These cookies are usually placed through third-party advertising networks.

### Third-party cookies

Some of the cookies used on our website are from third parties, such as:

- **Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics):** These cookies help us analyse how users use our site.
- **Social media platforms (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter):** These cookies are used when you interact with our social media features, such as sharing products on social media.

### Your choices

You have the option to manage your cookie preferences. Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to disable certain types of cookies, but this may affect your ability to use certain features on our website.

- **Browser settings:** You can set or amend your web browser to accept or reject cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, please be aware that some features of our website may not work as well.
- **Cookie Consent Tool:** When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent tool where you can choose which categories of cookies you agree to. You can update your preferences at any time by visiting our Cookie Policy page.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The date of the latest version will be indicated on the policy page. We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we use cookies.
