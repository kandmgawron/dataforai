## Privacy Policy Summary for Meridian Outdoor Co.

### Introduction 

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy Summary explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our 12 stores across Scotland and Northern England, our website, or any of our services. By engaging with us, you consent to the practices described here.

### Information We Collect

**Personal Identification Information**

- **When you place an order or make an inquiry:** We collect details like your name, address, email address, and phone number.
- **When you create an account:** We ask for a username and password, along with your contact information.
- **When you subscribe to our newsletter or promotions:** We collect your email address and any preferences you indicate.

**Payment Information**

- **When you make a purchase:** We collect payment details such as credit/debit card numbers, expiration dates, and security codes. We use payment processors to handle this data securely and do not store these details ourselves.

**Technical Information**

- **When you visit our website:** We collect technical data like your IP address, browser type, operating system, and browsing behaviour through cookies and analytics tools.

**Marketing and Communications Data**

- **When you engage with our marketing emails or promotions:** We collect information on how you interact with our communications, including whether you open emails or click links.

### How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

- **Processing Orders:** To fulfil your purchases, process payments, and deliver products or services.
- **Customer Service:** To respond to your inquiries, provide support, and resolve issues.
- **Account Management:** To manage your account, update you on new features or services, and ensure the security of your information.
- **Marketing and Promotions:** To send you promotional emails, newsletters, and other marketing materials based on your preferences and interests.
- **Improvements and Analysis:** To analyse how our website and services are used, improve our offerings, and develop new products or services.
- **Legal Compliance:** To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including fraud prevention and tax requirements.

### Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

- **Service Providers:** Third-party companies that provide services on our behalf, such as payment processors, delivery services, and customer support tools. These providers are contractually obligated to keep your data confidential and use it only for the services they provide.
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, we may disclose your information to comply with legal processes, such as court orders or regulatory requests.

### Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Access:** You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction:** You can ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Deletion:** In some cases, you can request that we delete your personal data.
- **Restriction:** You can ask us to restrict the processing of your data in certain situations.
- **Transfer:** You have the right to transfer your data to another organisation under certain conditions.
- **Object:** You can object to the processing of your data for direct marketing purposes.
