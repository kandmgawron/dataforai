## Meridian Outdoor Co. Complaints Procedure

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we are committed to providing our customers with the highest quality outdoor equipment and clothing, alongside exceptional customer service. We recognise that occasionally issues may arise, and we have established a clear and straightforward complaints procedure to address any concerns you may have. This procedure applies to all our 12 stores located across Scotland and Northern England.

### How to Lodge a Complaint

**In-Store Complaints**

If you encounter an issue while shopping in one of our stores, please follow these steps:

- **Speak to a Member of Staff:** Immediately inform a member of staff about your concern. They will do their best to resolve the issue on the spot.
- **Escalate to a Manager:** If the issue cannot be resolved at the first stage, ask to speak to a store manager. They will provide further assistance and guide you through the next steps.

**Complaints via Post or Email**

If you prefer to lodge a complaint via post or email, please use the following contact details:

- **Postal Address:** 
  ```plaintext
  Customer Services
  Meridian Outdoor Co.
  123 High Street
  Edinburgh
  EH1 1LD
  ```
  
- **Email Address:** complaints@meridianoutdoor.co.uk

When submitting a complaint, please include the following information to help us process your request efficiently:

- Your full name and contact details
- A detailed description of the issue
- Date and location of the purchase or service
- Any relevant receipts or order numbers

### Handling Your Complaint

Once we receive your complaint, our Customer Services team will acknowledge it within **3 working days**. We will then investigate the issue and provide a formal response within **10 working days**. In complex cases, we may take longer, but we will keep you informed of any delays.

### Our Commitment to Resolution

We aim to resolve all complaints fairly and promptly. Our responses may include:

- An apology and explanation where appropriate
- A refund, repair, or replacement of faulty goods
- A service redo or compensation where services have not met our standards
- Other appropriate remedies as per consumer law

### Feedback and Follow-Up

We value your feedback and view complaints as opportunities to improve our service. If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may request a follow-up meeting with a senior member of our Customer Services team.
