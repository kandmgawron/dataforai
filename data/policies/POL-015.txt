## Sleeping Bag Care & Storage Guidelines

Proper care and storage of your sleeping bag are essential to maintain its performance, comfort, and longevity. Follow these guidelines from Meridian Outdoor Co. to ensure your sleeping bag remains in excellent condition for your adventures.

### Cleaning Your Sleeping Bag

**Hand Washing**

- **Materials:** If your sleeping bag is made from delicate materials such as down or goose skin, hand washing is recommended.
- **Method:** Fill a basin with lukewarm water and a mild detergent. Gently squeeze the sleeping bag to clean it, avoiding vigorous scrubbing.
- **Rinsing:** Rinse thoroughly with clean lukewarm water until all soap is removed.
- **Drying:** Lay flat on a clean, dry towel in a well-ventilated area. Avoid direct sunlight. Allow to air dry completely before use or storage.

**Machine Washing**

- **Suitable Bags:** Only wash synthetic-filled sleeping bags in a washing machine.
- **Settings:** Use a gentle cycle with lukewarm water and a mild detergent.
- **Drying:** Use a low-heat setting on the tumble dryer or air dry as described above.

**Important Notes**

- **Avoid:** Do not use fabric softeners, bleach, or harsh chemicals.
- **Stains:** For stubborn stains, consider professional cleaning services.

### Drying Your Sleeping Bag

- **Air Drying:** The best method for drying your sleeping bag is to air dry it. Lay it flat on a clean, dry surface in a well-ventilated area, away from direct sunlight and heat sources.
- **Avoid:** Do not use high heat, as this can damage the filling and seams.
- **Hanging:** You can hang the sleeping bag from the hood to allow for better air circulation.

### Storing Your Sleeping Bag

**Compression Sacks**

- **Use:** Always store your sleeping bag in its compression sack when not in use. This helps maintain its loft and prevents compression that can reduce insulation properties.
- **Care:** Do not over-compress the bag, as this can permanently damage the filling.

**Storage Conditions**

- **Environment:** Store your sleeping bag in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight, moisture, and pests.
- **Avoid:** Do not store in basements or damp areas, as moisture can cause mould and deteriorate the materials.

### Regular Maintenance

- **Fluffing:** Regularly fluff and shake out your sleeping bag to evenly distribute the filling and maintain its insulation properties.
- **Inspection:** Periodically check for any signs of wear, tear, or damage to seams and zippers. Address any issues promptly to prevent further problems.

### Professional Cleaning

- **When Needed:** If your sleeping bag is excessively dirty or has persistent stains, consider professional cleaning services. Meridian Outdoor Co. recommends trusted local cleaners experienced with outdoor gear.

By following these care and storage guidelines, you will ensure that your Meridian Outdoor Co. sleeping bag remains in top condition, ready for your next adventure in the great outdoors.
