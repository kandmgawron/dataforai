## Extended Warranty Programme

### Introduction

Welcome to the Meridian Outdoor Co. Extended Warranty Programme. As a valued customer, we’re pleased to offer you this comprehensive protection plan designed to safeguard your favourite outdoor gear and clothing. This programme provides peace of mind, ensuring your equipment remains in top condition for years to come.

### Programme Benefits

Our Extended Warranty Programme offers several key benefits:

- **Extended Coverage:** Extend the manufacturer’s warranty by up to three years from the date of purchase.
- **Comprehensive Protection:** Covers manufacturing defects, material faults, and mechanical failures.
- **No Excess Fees:** Enjoy repair or replacement services without additional excess charges.
- **Convenient Service:** Benefit from hassle-free repairs at any of our 12 stores across Scotland and Northern England.
- **Priority Service:** Receive priority scheduling for repairs, minimising downtime for your essential gear.

### Eligibility

To be eligible for our Extended Warranty Programme, your purchase must meet the following criteria:

- **Valid Receipt:** A valid receipt showing the purchase date and product details.
- **Registration:** Register your product online or in-store within 30 days of purchase.
- **Eligible Items:** The programme covers most outdoor equipment and clothing brands, excluding items subject to normal wear and tear, accidental damage, or misuse.

### Registration Process

Registering your product is quick and easy. Follow these simple steps:

1. **Visit Our Website:** Go to our official website, meridianoutdoor.co.uk.
2. **Navigate to Warranty Registration:** Click on the “Warranty Registration” tab.
3. **Enter Product Details:** Input your product details and purchase information.
4. **Submit:** Complete the registration form and submit it online. Alternatively, you can register in-store by presenting your receipt to our customer service team.

### Making a Claim

If you need to make a claim under the Extended Warranty Programme, please follow these steps:

1. **Contact Customer Service:** Call our dedicated warranty line at 0800 123 4567 or visit any of our stores.
2. **Provide Details:** Present your valid receipt and describe the issue with your product.
3. **Assessment:** Our technical team will assess the problem and advise on the necessary repair or replacement.
4. **Repair or Replacement:** We will either repair the product or, if necessary, provide a replacement item of equivalent value.

### Terms and Conditions

Please note the following terms and conditions apply to the Extended Warranty Programme:

- **Duration:** The extended warranty period begins from the date of purchase and lasts for up to three years, depending on the product.
- **Exclusions:** The programme does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, normal wear and tear, or commercial use.
- **Transferability:** The warranty is transferable to a new owner if the product is resold within the warranty period.
- **Amendments:** Meridian Outdoor Co. reserves the right to amend or terminate the programme with reasonable notice.
