## Price Match Guarantee

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we are committed to providing our customers with the best value for money on all our outdoor equipment and clothing. We understand that price transparency and fairness are essential when making purchasing decisions, which is why we are proud to offer our Price Match Guarantee. 

This guarantee ensures that if you find the exact same product sold by a UK competitor, we will match their price, so you don't have to look further than Meridian Outdoor Co. for the best deal.

### How Our Price Match Guarantee Works

To qualify for our Price Match Guarantee, the following conditions must be met:

- **Identical Product:** The product must be the same brand, model, and condition (new or used) as the one available at Meridian Outdoor Co. This includes the same specifications, features, and packaging. 
- **UK Competitor:** The competing retailer must be a UK-based business operating within the UK. Online retailers, physical stores, and price comparison websites are all acceptable, provided they are UK-based.
- **Available Stock:** The competitor must have the product in stock and available for immediate purchase. We cannot price match on backordered or pre-order items.
- **Valid Proof of Price:** You must provide valid proof of the lower price. This can be a screenshot, printed advertisement, or a direct link to the competitor's website showing the lower price. The price must be clearly displayed and include all applicable taxes and delivery charges.
- **Same Purchase Conditions:** The competitor's price must include all applicable delivery charges, installation fees, and any other additional costs. We will only match the total price the competitor is charging.

### How to Claim Our Price Match Guarantee

To claim our Price Match Guarantee, please follow these simple steps:

1. **Find the Lower Price:** Locate the identical product at a lower price from a UK competitor.
2. **Gather Proof:** Obtain clear and valid proof of the lower price.
3. **Visit Us:** Bring the proof of the lower price to any of our 12 stores across Scotland and Northern England, or contact our customer service team via phone or email.
4. **Present Your Proof:** Our staff will review the proof and, if it meets our Price Match Guarantee conditions, we will match the competitor's price.
5. **Receive Your Discount:** Once approved, we will apply the price match to your purchase immediately.

### Exclusions

Our Price Match Guarantee does not apply to:

- **Clearance Items:** Products that are marked as clearance, outlet, or already discounted items.
- **Special Offers:** Products included in a special promotion, bundle offer, or discount event.
- **Third-Party Prices:** Prices found on third-party websites or auction sites that are not authorised retailers.
- **Non-Identical Products:** Products that are similar but not identical in brand, model, or specifications.
