## Merino Wool Care Guide

### Introduction

Welcome to the Merino Wool Care Guide from Meridian Outdoor Co. Our commitment to providing high-quality outdoor gear extends to ensuring the longevity of your merino wool garments. Proper care not only preserves the unique qualities of merino wool but also enhances its performance and durability. Follow these guidelines to keep your merino wool clothing in top condition.

### Understanding Merino Wool

Merino wool is renowned for its softness, breathability, and odour resistance. These qualities make it an excellent choice for outdoor apparel. However, to maintain its benefits, specific care is required.

### Washing Your Merino Wool

**Preparation**

- **Remove Odours:** If your garment has odours, sprinkle a bit of baking soda inside and leave it overnight to absorb smells before washing.
- **Check Labels:** Always check the care label for specific instructions.

**Washing Instructions**

- **Use Mild Detergent:** Use a mild, wool-specific detergent. Avoid regular laundry detergents, fabric softeners, or bleach, as these can damage the fibres.
- **Cold Water:** Wash in cold water. Hot water can shrink merino wool and reduce its effectiveness.
- **Gentle Cycle:** Use the gentle or delicate cycle on your washing machine. Avoid agitation, which can cause felting.
- **Load Size:** Wash garments inside out with similar colours and fabrics to prevent colour bleeding and damage.

### Drying Your Merino Wool

**Air Drying**

- **Flat Drying:** Lay your garment flat on a clean, dry surface to air dry. Avoid hanging, as this can cause stretching and loss of shape.
- **Avoid Direct Sunlight:** Keep the garment out of direct sunlight, as UV rays can fade the fabric over time.

**Machine Drying**

- **Avoid Tumble Drying:** Never tumble dry merino wool. The heat and agitation can irreversibly damage the fibres, causing shrinkage and loss of softness.

### Ironing and Steaming

- **Cool Iron:** If necessary, use a cool setting on your iron. Always test on a small, inconspicuous area first.
- **Steaming:** Steaming is a safer alternative to ironing and can help remove wrinkles without direct heat contact.

### Storage Tips

- **Fold, Don’t Hang:** Fold your merino wool garments to maintain their shape and prevent stretching. Use padded hangers if you must hang them, and remove them as soon as possible.
- **Clean Storage:** Store in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture.

### Spot Cleaning

- **Gentle Wipes:** For small stains, use a damp cloth with a few drops of wool detergent to gently dab the area. Avoid scrubbing, as this can damage the fibres.

### Conclusion

By following these care guidelines, you can ensure that your merino wool garments from Meridian Outdoor Co. remain soft, comfortable, and performant for many adventures to come. Thank you for choosing Meridian Outdoor Co., where quality and care go hand in hand.
