## Meridian Outdoor Co. Standard Returns Policy

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we aim to ensure that every customer is completely satisfied with their purchase. We understand that sometimes things may not go as expected, and we’re here to make the return process as easy and hassle-free as possible. Please read our standard returns policy carefully before making your purchase.

### Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return or exchange, the following conditions must be met:

- **Timeframe:** Items must be returned within 14 days of the date of purchase. This timeframe includes online and in-store purchases.
- **Condition:** Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in a resalable condition. Any item that has been worn, washed, or damaged may not be eligible for a refund.
- **Proof of Purchase:** A valid receipt or proof of purchase must be presented at the time of return. This can be a till receipt, online order confirmation email, or any other form of purchase proof.

### Return Process

#### Online Purchases

1. **Initiating Your Return:** To return an item purchased online, please log into your account on our website and follow the returns procedure, or alternatively contact our customer service team for assistance.
2. **Returns Portal / Label:** Once your return request is processed, you will receive a returns portal login or label via email.
3. **Sending Back the Item:** Pack the item securely in its original packaging, and attach the returns portal login or label to your parcel. Send it using a tracked service to the address provided in your return authorisation email. You are responsible for arranging and paying for the return postage.
4. **Refund/Exchange:** Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund or exchange. If approved, your refund will be processed, and credit issued automatically to your original method of payment within 5 working days.

#### In-Store Purchases

1. **Returning Items:** Bring the item, along with your receipt, to any of our 12 store locations across Scotland and Northern England.
2. **Assessment:** Our staff will assess the item to ensure it meets our return policy conditions.
3. **Refund/Exchange:** If the item is eligible, we will process your refund or exchange immediately. Refunds will be issued to the original method of payment.

### Refunds

- **Timing:** Refunds will be processed within 5 working days of us receiving and inspecting the returned item.
- **Method:** Refunds will be issued to the original method of payment. Please ensure that your bank details are up to date to avoid any delays.

### Exchanges

- **Process:** Exchanges can be requested either online or in-store, subject to availability of the requested item. If the requested item is out of stock, a refund will be issued.
- **Additional Costs:** If the value of the exchanged item is higher, any additional cost will be charged to you. If the value is lower, the difference will be refunded to you.

### Non-Returnable Items

Certain items are non-returnable for hygiene reasons, including but not limited to underwear, socks and swimwear where the original packaging has been opened.
