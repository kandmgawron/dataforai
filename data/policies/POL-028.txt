## Corporate & Group Orders

### Introduction

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we understand that businesses, schools, and group organisers often seek quality outdoor equipment and clothing for their members or employees. We are proud to offer a tailored corporate and group order service designed to meet your specific needs, ensuring you receive the right gear for your activities, events, or uniform requirements.

### Benefits of Choosing Meridian Outdoor Co.

- **Expertise**: With 12 stores across Scotland and Northern England, we have extensive experience in outdoor retail, ensuring you receive expert advice and support.
- **Custom Solutions**: We offer bespoke order solutions to suit your group's size, budget, and specific requirements.
- **Competitive Pricing**: Benefit from bulk order discounts and special pricing arrangements for large orders.
- **Efficient Service**: Our dedicated corporate sales team will guide you through the ordering process, from initial enquiry to delivery.

### How to Place a Corporate or Group Order

#### Step 1: Initial Enquiry

To begin, please contact our Corporate Sales Team via email at **corporate@meridianoutdoor.co.uk** or call us at **0800 123 4567**. Provide the following details:

- **Group Name and Contact Information**: Including name, address, phone number, and email.
- **Nature of the Order**: Details about the type of equipment or clothing needed, the event or activity it is for, and the expected order size.

#### Step 2: Consultation and Quote

Our team will schedule a consultation to discuss your requirements in detail. We will:

- **Assess Your Needs**: Understand your specific requirements and preferences.
- **Provide Recommendations**: Suggest suitable products based on your needs and budget.
- **Offer a Custom Quote**: Provide a detailed quote outlining product choices, pricing, and any available discounts.

#### Step 3: Order Placement

Once you have approved the quote, we will proceed with order placement. You will receive:

- **Order Confirmation**: A detailed confirmation email outlining the products, quantities, pricing, and expected delivery date.
- **Payment Terms**: Information on payment options, including invoice, credit card, or other accepted methods.

#### Step 4: Production and Delivery

We will efficiently process your order and ensure timely delivery. You will receive:

- **Production Updates**: Regular updates on the status of your order.
- **Delivery Arrangements**: Coordination of delivery to your specified address, with options for tracking your order.

### Additional Services

- **Branding and Customisation**: We offer branding services for uniforms and equipment, ensuring your group’s logo or design is prominently displayed.
- **Rental Options**: For short-term needs, we provide rental services for a variety of outdoor equipment.
- **On-Site Visits**: For large orders or specific requirements, we offer on-site visits to discuss your needs in person.
