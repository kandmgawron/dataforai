## FAQs: Returns & Exchanges

### **What is Meridian Outdoor Co.'s returns policy?**

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you are not, we offer a straightforward returns policy. You can return most items purchased online or in-store within **30 days** of the delivery date for a refund, exchange, or credit.

### **How do I return an item purchased online?**

To return an item purchased online, please follow these steps:

1. **Initiate your return**: Log in to your account on our website and go to the "Returns" section. Select the items you wish to return and follow the instructions to generate a returns authorisation.
2. **Packaging**: Ensure the item is in its original packaging and condition, with all tags and labels attached. 
3. **Postage**: We recommend using a tracked postal service for returning items. You are responsible for the cost of returning the item unless the item is faulty or incorrect. 
4. **Submit your return**: Send the packaged item to the address provided in your returns authorisation. Once we receive and inspect the item, we will process your return.

### **Can I return items purchased in-store?**

Yes, you can return items purchased in-store by visiting any of our 12 stores across Scotland and Northern England. Please bring the item, along with your receipt, to the store. Our staff will assist you with the return process.

### **What is the process for exchanging an item?**

Exchanges are easy and can be done either online or in-store:

1. **Online exchanges**: Log in to your account, go to the "Returns" section, and select "Exchange" for the item you wish to exchange. Choose your new size or colour and follow the instructions to generate an exchange authorisation. We will provide you with a new item free of charge, or arrange a collection if preferred. 
2. **In-store exchanges**: Visit any of our stores with the item and your receipt. Our staff will assist you with exchanging the item for the size or colour you prefer.

### **What items are not eligible for return or exchange?**

Certain items are not eligible for return or exchange, including:

- **Personalised items**: Any item that has been personalised or customised.
- **Perishable goods**: Items that are perishable by nature, such as food or drink.
- **Downloadable software**: Any digital products or downloadable software.

### **How will I receive my refund?**

Refunds will be processed using the original payment method unless otherwise agreed. Please allow 2-4 working days for the refund to appear in your account after we receive and inspect the returned item.

### **What if my item is faulty?**

If you receive a faulty item, please contact our customer service team as soon as possible. We will arrange for the item to be repaired, replaced, or refunded, depending on the situation. In all cases, the customer does **not** need to pay return postage if the item is at fault.
