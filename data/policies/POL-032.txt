## **Helmets & Safety Equipment Policy**

### **Introduction**

At Meridian Outdoor Co., the safety and well-being of our customers and staff are our top priorities. This Helmets & Safety Equipment Policy outlines our commitment to ensuring that all helmets and safety equipment sold in our stores meet the highest standards of quality, safety, and performance. This policy applies to all 12 Meridian Outdoor Co. stores located across Scotland and Northern England.

### **Policy Objectives**

- **Ensure Compliance:** All helmets and safety equipment must comply with relevant British Standards (BS) and European Norm (EN) regulations.
- **Maintain Quality:** We will only stock products from reputable manufacturers that adhere to strict quality control measures.
- **Customer Safety:** Provide accurate information and guidance to customers to help them select the appropriate safety equipment for their needs.
- **Staff Training:** Equip our staff with the knowledge and skills to advise customers effectively on helmet and safety equipment use.

### **Compliance Standards**

All helmets and safety equipment sold by Meridian Outdoor Co. must meet the following standards:

- **Cycling Helmets:** EN 1400 or BS EN 406
- **Ski and Snowboard Helmets:** EN 398
- **Hiking and Trekking Helmets:** EN 403
- **Other Safety Equipment:** Compliance with relevant BS or EN standards, as applicable

### **Product Selection and Verification**

- **Vendor Approval:** Only purchase helmets and safety equipment from approved vendors who provide certification of compliance with the above standards.
- **Regular Audits:** Conduct regular audits of our product range to ensure ongoing compliance.
- **Label Verification:** Ensure all products are clearly labelled with the appropriate certification marks and safety information.

### **Staff Training and Information**

- **Initial Training:** All staff will receive comprehensive training on the features, benefits, and correct usage of helmets and safety equipment.
- **Ongoing Education:** Regular refresher courses and updates will be provided to keep staff informed about the latest safety standards and product innovations.
- **Product Knowledge:** Staff must be able to identify key features, proper fit, and maintenance requirements of all safety equipment.

### **Customer Guidance**

Meridian Outdoor Co. staff are authorised to provide the following guidance to customers:

- **Correct Sizing and Fit:** Demonstrate proper fitting techniques to ensure the helmet fits snugly and comfortably.
- **Usage Instructions:** Provide information on the appropriate use of the equipment for the intended activity.
- **Maintenance Advice:** Advise customers on how to inspect and maintain their helmets and safety equipment to ensure ongoing effectiveness.
- **Safety Tips:** Offer general safety tips and best practices related to the use of helmets and other safety gear.

### **Reporting and Recalls**

- **Incident Reporting:** Staff must report any incidents or concerns regarding the safety or performance of helmets and safety equipment to the store manager immediately.
- **Recall Procedure:** In the event of a product recall, store managers will follow the manufacturer's instructions to inform affected customers and arrange for the return or replacement of the product.

### **Conclusion**

This Helmets & Safety Equipment Policy reflects Meridian Outdoor Co.'s dedication to providing safe, high-quality products and expert advice to our customers. By adhering to these guidelines, we ensure that our customers can enjoy their outdoor activities with confidence, knowing they are protected by the best available safety equipment.
