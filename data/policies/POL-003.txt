## In-Store Returns & Exchanges Policy

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we strive to ensure that every customer is satisfied with their purchase. We understand that sometimes things may not go as expected, and we are here to assist you with your in-store returns and exchanges. Please read our policy carefully to understand your rights and options.

### Returns

**Timeframe**

- **Standard Returns:** Customers have 14 days from the date of purchase to return items for an exchange, credit, or refund, provided the items are in a resalable condition and accompanied by a till receipt.

**Conditions**

- **Resalable Condition:** Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all tags attached. Clothing must have the size tags attached. Equipment must be clean and free from odour or damage.
- **Proof of Purchase:** A till receipt must be presented at the point of return. We cannot process returns without proof of purchase.

**Refund Options**

- **Credit:** We offer store credit for future purchases, valid for 12 months from the date of issue.
- **Refund:** Refunds will be processed to the original payment method. Please allow 2-4 working days for the refund to appear in your account.

### Exchanges

**Process**

- **Immediate Service:** Exchanges can be processed instantly, provided the item is in a resalable condition and the desired exchange is available in-store.
- **Different Size/Colour:** If the desired size or colour is not available, we will gladly place an order for you. The exchange will be processed when the item becomes available. There may be a wait time depending on stock levels.

**Conditions**

- **Resalable Condition:** As with returns, items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all tags attached.
- **Proof of Purchase:** A till receipt must be presented.

### Faulty or Incorrect Items

**Procedure**

- **Report Immediately:** If you receive a faulty item or an incorrect item, please report it to a member of staff immediately. We will guide you through the necessary steps for repair, replacement, or refund.
- **Warranty:** Items purchased from Meridian Outdoor Co. may be covered by the manufacturer's warranty. Our staff will help you understand and utilise this warranty.

### Contact Us

Should you require any further assistance with your return or exchange, please do not hesitate to contact the store where you made your purchase. Our staff are trained to provide helpful and efficient service to ensure your satisfaction.

We thank you for choosing Meridian Outdoor Co. and we look forward to serving you again soon.
