## **Product Recall Procedure**

### **Introduction**

This product recall procedure outlines the steps Meridian Outdoor Co. will take to manage product recalls effectively and efficiently. This ensures the safety of our customers and maintains our reputation as a trusted retailer of outdoor equipment and clothing.

### **Recall Initiation**

**Identification of a Defect**

- **Monitoring:** Our Quality Assurance Team will continually monitor product performance, customer feedback, and reports from our stores.
- **Supplier Notifications:** We will maintain open communication with suppliers to be immediately informed of any potential defects or recalls.
- **Internal Audits:** Regular internal audits will be conducted to identify any potential issues with products on our shelves.

**Decision to Recall**

- **Assessment:** Upon identification of a potential defect, our Quality Assurance Team will assess the risk to customers.
- **Consultation:** We will consult with suppliers, health and safety experts, and relevant authorities such as the UK government’s Product Safety Colour Gate before initiating a recall.
- **Approval:** The final decision to recall a product will be approved by the Head of Quality Assurance and the Store Operations Manager.

### **Recall Execution**

**Communication Plan**

- **Internal Communication:** Immediate notification will be sent to all store managers and online sales team via email and our internal messaging system.
- **Customer Communication:** We will issue a public statement on our website, social media platforms, and in-store notices. An email and SMS alert will be sent to customers who have purchased the product.

**Steps for Stores**

- **Identification:** Store staff will be provided with a clear list of product details (e.g., SKU, model numbers, batch numbers) to identify the recalled items.
- **Removal:** All recalled products will be removed from shelves immediately. A designated area will be set up for quarantined items.
- **Customer Assistance:** Staff will be trained to inform customers about the recall, offer assistance, and provide information on how to return or exchange the product.

**Online Orders**

- **Order Suspension:** Any pending orders for the recalled product will be suspended.
- **Returns Portal:** An easy-to-use returns portal will be set up on our website for customers to return the product. We will offer a full refund or replacement as appropriate.
- **Customer Service:** Our customer service team will be prepared to handle inquiries related to the recall, providing clear guidance on returns and refunds.

### **Documentation and Reporting**

**Record Keeping**

- **Log:** A detailed log of the recall will be maintained, including the date of initiation, products involved, communication methods used, and customer responses.
- **Incident Reports:** Incident reports will be completed for each store and the online platform, documenting the number of products returned, customer complaints, and any issues encountered during the recall process.

**Post-Recall Review**

- **Analysis:** After the recall is complete, our Quality Assurance Team will analyse the effectiveness of the recall procedure.
- **Improvement:** Based on the analysis, we will implement any necessary changes to improve future recall processes.
- **Reporting:** A final report will be submitted to senior management, summarising the recall process, outcomes, and recommendations for improvement.

### **Conclusion**

This product recall procedure ensures that Meridian Outdoor Co. responds promptly and professionally to any product defects, safeguarding our customers and maintaining our commitment to quality and safety.
