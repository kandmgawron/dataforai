## Express & Next Day Delivery Policy for Meridian Outdoor Co.

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we understand that your outdoor adventures shouldn't be delayed. That's why we offer reliable and efficient express and next-day delivery options to ensure you receive your essential gear when you need it.

### Express Delivery

Our express delivery service is designed for customers who require their orders urgently. With this option, your carefully selected outdoor equipment and clothing will be dispatched from our warehouse within 24 hours of your order being confirmed. 

**Key features of our express delivery service include:**

- **Fast dispatch:** Orders are processed and dispatched within 24 hours.
- **Delivery timeframe:** Your order will be delivered to your doorstep within 1-2 business days, depending on your location.
- **Order cut-off time:** To qualify for express delivery, orders must be placed before 3 PM Monday to Friday. Orders placed after this time will be dispatched on the next business day.
- **Tracking information:** Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and a link to track your delivery in real-time.
- **Delivery areas:** Express delivery is available to all areas within the UK, including Scotland and Northern England, where our stores are located.

### Next Day Delivery

For those who need their gear even faster, our next-day delivery service is the perfect solution. This option guarantees that your order will be with you the very next business day after dispatch.

**Key features of our next-day delivery service include:**

- **Fast dispatch:** Orders are processed and dispatched on the same day, usually within the same business day of ordering.
- **Delivery timeframe:** Your order will be delivered to your doorstep the next business day after dispatch.
- **Order cut-off time:** To qualify for next-day delivery, orders must be placed before 11 AM Monday to Friday. Orders placed after this time will be delivered on the following business day.
- **Tracking information:** Similar to our express delivery service, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and a link to track your delivery in real-time.
- **Delivery areas:** Next-day delivery is available to all areas within the UK, including Scotland and Northern England.

### Important Notes

- **Business days:** Our delivery services operate on business days (Monday to Friday). Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.
- **Delivery times:** While we aim to deliver within the specified timeframes, please note that delivery times can occasionally be affected by external factors such as severe weather, traffic conditions, or issues with the courier service. 
- **Delivery charges:** Delivery charges for express and next-day delivery will be clearly displayed at the checkout. These charges vary depending on the delivery option selected and the total weight and dimensions of your order.
- **Customer service:** If you encounter any issues with your delivery, or if you have any questions about our delivery services, please contact our customer service team. We are here to help ensure your experience with Meridian Outdoor Co. is seamless and satisfactory.

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we are committed to providing you with the best possible service, ensuring that you are fully equipped for your outdoor adventures, right when you need them.
