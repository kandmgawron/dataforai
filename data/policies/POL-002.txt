## Online Order Returns Policy

Thank you for shopping with Meridian Outdoor Co. We are committed to ensuring your satisfaction with every purchase. If you need to return an item purchased online, please follow the guidelines below.

### **Eligibility for Returns**

To be eligible for a return, please ensure that:

- **Items are unused and in their original packaging** with all tags attached.
- **The return is initiated within 14 days** of receiving your order.
- **Proof of purchase** (order confirmation email) is available.

### **Non-Returnable Items**

Certain items are not eligible for return, including:

- **Personalised or custom-made items.**
- **Perishable goods.**
- **Opened hygiene products.**

### **How to Initiate a Return**

Follow these steps to initiate your return:

1. **Log in to Your Account:** Access your account on our website.
2. **Go to 'My Orders':** Locate the order you wish to return under 'My Orders'.
3. **Select 'Return Items':** Choose the items you wish to return and follow the on-screen instructions.
4. **Print the Return Label:** You will receive a return shipping label. Print this label and attach it to your parcel.

### **Return Shipping Costs**

- **Standard Returns:** Free of charge.
- **Incorrect or Defective Items:** We will cover the return shipping costs.
- **Change of Mind Returns:** Free of charge.

### **Refunds**

Once we receive and inspect your returned items, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit issued to your original method of payment within 5 working days.

### **Exchanges**

If you would like to exchange an item for a different size or colour, please follow the return process outlined above and select 'Exchange' during the process. Please note that exchanges are subject to availability.
