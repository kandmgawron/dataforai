## Footwear Care & Waterproofing

### Introduction

Maintaining the condition and performance of your footwear is essential for longevity and optimal performance, especially when facing the unpredictable British weather. Meridian Outdoor Co. provides expert advice to help you care for and waterproof your footwear effectively.

### General Care Tips

To ensure your footwear remains in top condition, follow these general care tips:

- **Regular Cleaning:** Clean your footwear regularly to remove dirt and debris. Use a soft, damp cloth and a mild cleaner suitable for the material of your shoes. Avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive tools, as these can damage the material.
- **Drying Properly:** Never leave wet footwear to dry inside a heater or in direct sunlight, as this can cause the material to shrink or warp. Instead, air-dry your footwear naturally, preferably upside down, to maintain their shape.
- **Removing Odour:** To eliminate unpleasant odours, sprinkle baking soda inside your footwear and leave it overnight. Alternatively, use an odour-neutralising spray specifically designed for footwear.
- **Rotating Footwear:** Rotate between pairs of footwear to allow them adequate time to air out and maintain their structure.

### Waterproofing Your Footwear

Waterproofing your footwear is crucial for keeping your feet dry in wet conditions. Follow these steps for effective waterproofing:

#### **Before Use:**

- **Waterproof Sprays:** Apply a waterproof spray to the exterior of your footwear. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for the best results. For optimal protection, reapply every few months or after heavy use.
- **Wax Treatments:** For leather footwear, consider using a wax treatment. Apply the wax evenly to the surface of the leather, then buff it in with a soft cloth. This adds a protective layer and enhances the leather's natural water resistance.

#### **During Use:**

- **Waterproof Socks:** Wear waterproof socks under your footwear for added protection. These socks act as an extra barrier against moisture and can help keep your feet dry in extreme conditions.
- **Gaiters:** In very wet conditions, use gaiters to cover the top of your footwear and prevent water from entering through the openings.

#### **After Use:**

- **Cleaning and Reapplication:** After prolonged use in wet conditions, clean your footwear thoroughly and reapply your waterproof spray or wax treatment to maintain its effectiveness.
