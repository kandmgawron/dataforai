### Programme Membership

**Eligibility:** The Meridian Loyalty Programme is open to individuals aged 18 and above who are residents of the UK. 

**Registration:** To become a member, you must complete the registration process online at our website or in-store. You will need to provide accurate and complete information. You are responsible for keeping your account information up to date.

**Account:** Upon registration, you will receive a unique membership number. You must keep this number confidential and secure. 

### Earning Points

**Purchases:** You will earn loyalty points based on the total spend (before taxes and discounts) on qualifying purchases made in-store or online at Meridian Outdoor Co. The points awarded will be based on the current rewards structure, which is available on our website and in-store.

**Non-Qualifying Purchases:** Certain purchases may not qualify for points, including but not limited to: gift vouchers, warranty services and non-Meridian Outdoor Co. products. 

### Redeeming Points

**Eligibility:** Only registered members can redeem points. Points can be redeemed for rewards, discounts and exclusive offers as detailed on our website. 

**Redemption Process:** To redeem points, you must do so in accordance with the instructions provided on our website or in-store. Rewards are subject to availability and may change without notice.

**Expiry:** Points will expire 24 months from the date of acquisition unless otherwise stated. Expired points cannot be reinstated.
