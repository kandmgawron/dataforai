## FAQs: Loyalty Programme

### What is the Meridian Rewards Loyalty Programme?

The Meridian Rewards Loyalty Programme is an exclusive membership programme designed for our regular customers. It offers a range of benefits, including exclusive discounts, early access to sales, and points earning on every purchase that can be redeemed for rewards.

### How do I join the Meridian Rewards programme?

Joining the Meridian Rewards programme is simple and free. You can sign up in any of our 12 stores across Scotland and Northern England or online at meridianoutdoor.co.uk. All you need is to provide your email address, and you'll start earning points immediately.

### How do I earn points with Meridian Rewards?

You earn points with every purchase you make at Meridian Outdoor Co. Here’s how it works:

- **Earn 1 point** for every £1 spent on full-price items.
- **Earn 2 points** for every £1 spent on sale items.
- **Additional points** may be earned through special promotions and events.

### How do I redeem my points?

Once you have accumulated enough points, you can redeem them for various rewards, including discounts, exclusive products, and special experiences. You can check your points balance and redeem them online or in any of our stores. 

### What rewards can I redeem my points for?

Rewards vary and are updated regularly to offer exciting options for our members. Common rewards include:

- **Discount vouchers** for future purchases.
- **Exclusive access** to new product launches.
- **Special event tickets**, such as product launches or outdoor workshops.
- **Merchandise**, including branded items like hats, bags, and water bottles.

### Are there any exclusive offers for loyalty members?

Yes, as a Meridian Rewards member, you will receive exclusive offers and early access to sales events. We also send out monthly newsletters with the latest deals, new arrivals, and member-only promotions.

### Is there an expiration date for my points?

Points earned through the Meridian Rewards programme do not expire as long as you continue to make a purchase at least once every 12 months. If 12 months pass without a purchase, your points balance will be reset to zero.

### Can I check my points balance online?

Yes, you can easily check your points balance and transaction history online by logging into your account on our website, meridianoutdoor.co.uk. You can also check your balance in any of our stores.

### What if I have a complaint or issue with the loyalty programme?

We strive to ensure a seamless experience for all our loyalty members. If you have any complaints or issues, please contact our customer service team at customerservice@meridianoutdoor.co.uk or visit any of our stores. We are here to help and will do our best to resolve any concerns promptly.

### Is the loyalty programme free?

Yes, the Meridian Rewards Loyalty Programme is completely free to join and has no membership fees.

### Can I gift my points to someone else?

Points cannot be transferred to another person’s account. However, you can use your points to purchase gifts for others!
