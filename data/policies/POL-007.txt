## **Jacket & Outerwear Sizing Guide**

### **Understanding Your Size**

Finding the perfect jacket or piece of outerwear is essential for comfort, protection, and performance, especially in the varied climates of Scotland and Northern England. This sizing guide from Meridian Outdoor Co. is designed to help you choose the right fit for our range of jackets and outerwear.

### **Sizing Chart**

Our jackets and outerwear typically come in the following sizes: S, M, L, XL, XXL. However, sizing can vary between styles and brands. Always refer to the specific size chart provided on the product page or in-store.

**General Size Guide:**

| **Size** | **Chest (inches)** | **Chest (cm)** |
| **S** | **34-36** | **86-91** |
| **M** | **38-40** | **96-101** |
| **L** | **42-44** | **106-111** |
| **XL** | **46-48** | **116-122** |
| **XXL** | **50-52** | **126-132** |

### **Measuring Yourself**

To ensure the best fit, measure your chest, waist, and inseam length before shopping:

**Chest:** Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms and over your shoulder blades.

**Waist:** Measure around your natural waistline, typically just above your hips.

**Inseam:** Measure from the inside of one ankle to the inside of the opposite leg at the crotch.

### **Fit Types**

We offer a variety of fits to suit different preferences:

**Regular Fit:** Our most common fit, offering a comfortable balance between loose and tight. Ideal for layering.

**Slim Fit:** A more tailored fit that hugs the body closely, tapering slightly at the waist and ankles. Perfect for those who prefer a streamlined look.

**Insulated Fit:** Designed for extra warmth, these jackets have additional padding. Sizes tend to run a bit larger to accommodate the insulation.

**Waterproof Fit:** These jackets are tailored to be snug to enhance waterproof and windproof performance. Opt for this fit if you'll be exposed to the elements.

### **Layering Consideration**

When planning to layer your outerwear, consider the following:

- Allow extra space for base and mid-layers, especially in colder weather.
- A regular fit jacket will usually accommodate an additional layer comfortably.
- If you plan to wear multiple layers, you may wish to size up from your usual size.

### **Try Before You Buy**

While this guide provides a good starting point, individual body shapes and personal preferences can vary. We recommend trying on jackets in-store if possible, where our knowledgeable staff can provide further assistance and ensure the perfect fit.
