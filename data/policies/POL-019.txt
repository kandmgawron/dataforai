## Meridian Outdoor Co. Click & Collect Service

### Introduction

Meridian Outdoor Co. is delighted to offer a convenient Click & Collect service for our valued customers. This service allows you to order online from the comfort of your home and collect your purchases from one of our 12 conveniently located stores across Scotland and Northern England. Our Click & Collect service is designed to provide you with a seamless shopping experience, ensuring that you get the outdoor equipment and clothing you need when you need it.

### How to Use Click & Collect

#### Step 1: Shopping Online

1. **Browse Our Website**: Visit our official website to browse our extensive range of outdoor equipment and clothing.
2. **Select Items**: Add the items you wish to purchase to your shopping cart.
3. **Choose Click & Collect**: At the checkout, select the 'Click & Collect' option.
4. **Select Store**: Choose one of our 12 stores as your preferred collection point.
5. **Review & Confirm**: Review your order details and confirm your purchase.

#### Step 2: Order Confirmation

- **Email Confirmation**: You will receive an email confirmation of your order, detailing the items purchased, the estimated collection time, and any additional information.
- **Order Tracking**: You can track your order status online using the tracking number provided in your confirmation email.

#### Step 3: Collecting Your Order

- **Collection Time**: Orders are typically ready for collection within 2-5 working days. You will receive an email notification when your order is ready.
- **Bring Identification**: To collect your order, please bring a valid form of identification and the email confirmation or order tracking number.
- **Store Hours**: Please ensure you visit your selected store during its operating hours. Our store hours are listed on our website.

### Benefits of Click & Collect

- **Convenience**: Shop online at your own pace and collect at a time that suits you.
- **Immediate Access**: Get your outdoor gear quickly without the hassle of delivery delays.
- **Flexibility**: Change or cancel your order online before it is processed.
- **Try Before You Buy**: Some items can be tried on in-store before you finalise your purchase.

### Frequently Asked Questions

- **Can I Change My Collection Store?** 
Yes, you can change your collection store up until your order is processed. Log in to your account and modify your order details.
- **What If I’m Running Late?**
If you are running late, please contact your selected store directly to inform them. They will hold your order for up to 30 minutes beyond the estimated collection time.
- **Can I Return Items Purchased Through Click & Collect?**
Yes, all items purchased through Click & Collect can be returned to any of our stores within 28 days of collection, provided they are in a resalable condition.
