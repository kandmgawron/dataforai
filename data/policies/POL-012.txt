## Waterproof Garment Care & Reproofing

### Introduction

Maintaining the waterproof integrity of your outdoor gear is essential for ensuring its longevity and performance. Meridian Outdoor Co. recommends following these guidelines to care for and reproof your waterproof garments, keeping you protected in any weather condition.

### General Care Instructions

To preserve the waterproof properties of your gear, adhere to these basic care instructions:

- **Read the Label:** Always check the care label on your garment for specific washing instructions.
- **Use Mild Detergents:** Avoid using fabric softeners, bleach, or harsh detergents. Opt for mild, eco-friendly detergents instead.
- **Wash Inside Out:** Turn your garment inside out before washing to protect the waterproof coating.
- **Low Heat:** Use low heat settings on your washing machine and avoid tumble drying. If tumble drying is necessary, use a low temperature setting and remove the garment promptly.
- **Air Dry:** Hang your garment in a well-ventilated area to air dry. Avoid direct sunlight, as UV rays can damage waterproof coatings.

### Tips for Extending Waterproof Performance

- **Regular Cleaning:** Keep your garments clean to prevent dirt and debris from compromising the waterproof coating.
- **Store Properly:** Store your gear in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures.
- **Avoid Abrasion:** Be mindful of potential abrasions that can damage the waterproof coating. When not in use, store garments hanging up or folded gently, avoiding sharp objects.
