## Highland & Island Delivery Surcharges

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we strive to provide our customers with efficient and reliable delivery services across the UK. However, due to the unique logistical challenges associated with delivering to the Highland and Island regions of Scotland, we have implemented specific surcharges to ensure continued service quality and affordability for all our customers.

### **Scope of Surcharges**

The following areas are affected by the Highland and Island delivery surcharges:

- **Highland Areas:** These include regions such as Inverness, Fort William, and surrounding areas.
- **Island Regions:** This covers all islands off the mainland, including but not limited to the Isle of Skye, Orkney, Shetland, and the Hebrides.

### **Surcharge Details**

To address the increased costs associated with delivering to these remote locations, we have established the following surcharge structure:

- **Standard Delivery Surcharge:** An additional £10 will be applied to all orders delivered to the specified Highland and Island regions.
- **Next-Day Delivery Surcharge:** For next-day delivery services, an additional £20 will be applied to orders in these areas.

### **Why These Surcharges?**

The surcharges are necessary due to several factors:

- **Increased Transport Costs:** The remote nature of these areas often requires specialised transport solutions and longer travel distances, significantly increasing our shipping expenses.
- **Limited Delivery Options:** There are fewer courier services operating in these regions, and those that do often charge premium rates for deliveries.
- **Infrastructure Challenges:** Weather conditions and terrain can impact delivery times and reliability, necessitating additional planning and resources.

### **Our Commitment**

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we remain committed to serving all our customers equitably. While these surcharges may affect some of our customers in the Highland and Island regions, they are essential to maintaining our delivery standards and ensuring we can continue to offer competitive pricing on our wide range of outdoor equipment and clothing.

We appreciate your understanding and continue to value your custom. If you have any questions regarding these surcharges or our delivery services, please do not hesitate to contact our customer service team.
