## Expedition & Specialist Equipment Advice

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we understand that preparing for an expedition or a specialised outdoor activity requires meticulous planning and the right equipment. Our expert staff are trained to assist you in selecting the most suitable gear to ensure safety, comfort, and enjoyment on your adventure. Below is a guide to help you navigate our range of specialist equipment and access our expert advice.

### **Navigation & Safety**

- **Maps & Compasses**: Essential for any expedition. We stock Ordnance Survey maps for Scotland and Northern England, as well as international maps for your global adventures. Pair your map with a high-quality compass from brands like Wild Country and Suunto.
- **GPS Devices**: For those who prefer digital navigation, we offer GPS devices that offer offline maps and tracking. Brands such as Garmin and Magellan provide reliable options.
- **Headlamps & Torches**: Safety is paramount. Our range includes headlamps with multiple beam options and durable torches. Look for models with waterproof ratings and long battery life.
- **First Aid Kits**: Comprehensive kits from reputable brands like Wild Country and Black Diamond, equipped for various scenarios.

### **Shelter & Sleep**

- **Tents**: From lightweight trekking tents to robust family-sized options, our selection caters to all needs. Brands such as Wild Country, Rab, and Wild Country offer innovative designs with features like breathable fabrics and secure storm-proof structures.
- **Sleeping Bags**: Choose from a variety of temperature ratings and styles. Our range includes down and synthetic fill bags from leading brands, ensuring you stay warm in varying conditions.
- **Sleeping Mats**: Essential for insulation and comfort. We offer single and double mats with different R-values to suit your sleeping needs.

### **Clothing & Footwear**

- **Base Layers**: High-performance base layers from brands like Darn Tough and Smartwool, designed to wick moisture and regulate temperature.
- **Insulation Layers**: Mid-layers including fleeces and insulated jackets from Rab and Wild Country to keep you warm.
- **Outerwear**: Waterproof and windproof jackets and trousers from leading brands such as Wild Country and Rab, ensuring you remain dry in harsh conditions.
- **Footwear**: From lightweight hiking boots to heavy-duty expedition boots, our range from brands like Scarpa and Merrell offers superior support and durability. Don’t forget moisture-wicking socks from Darn Tough.

### **Cooking & Hydration**

- **Portable Stoves**: Reliable cooking solutions from brands like MSR and Wild Country, including multi-fuel and canister stoves.
- **Cooking Systems**: Complete systems with pots, pans, and utensils. Our range includes lightweight and compact options for easy packing.
- **Hydration Systems**: Stay hydrated with water bottles, hydration bladders, and purification tablets. Brands such as MSR and Wild Country offer innovative solutions to keep you hydrated.

### **Expert Advice**

Our store experts are ready to provide personalised advice based on your specific expedition needs. Whether you’re planning a trek in the Scottish Highlands or a climbing expedition in the Alps, we’re here to help you prepare with the right equipment and knowledge.
