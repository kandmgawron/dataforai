## Environmental Commitment

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we understand that our business decisions have a significant impact on the environment. As a retailer dedicated to outdoor adventures, we recognise our responsibility to protect the natural spaces that we and our customers cherish. Our commitment to sustainability is not just a policy; it's a promise to our planet, our customers, and future generations.

### Our Vision

Our vision is to become a leading example of sustainable retail within the outdoor equipment and clothing sector. We aim to minimise our environmental footprint through responsible sourcing, reducing waste, and promoting eco-friendly practices across all aspects of our operations.

### Sustainable Sourcing

We are dedicated to sourcing products ethically and sustainably:

- **Responsible Materials:** We prioritises materials that are organic, recycled, or sustainably sourced. We partner with suppliers who adhere to strict environmental and ethical standards.
- **Fair Trade:** We support fair trade practices, ensuring that workers in our supply chain receive fair wages and work in safe conditions.

### Energy and Emissions

We are committed to reducing our energy consumption and carbon emissions:

- **Energy Efficiency:** We invest in energy-efficient lighting, heating, and cooling systems in our stores. We also utilise LED lighting to reduce energy usage.
- **Renewable Energy:** Where possible, we source renewable energy to power our stores. We are exploring opportunities to install solar panels at selected locations.
- **Carbon Offsetting:** We actively engage in carbon offsetting programmes to mitigate the emissions we cannot yet eliminate.

### Waste Reduction

We strive to minimise waste and promote a circular economy:

- **Packaging:** We reduce, reuse, and recycle packaging materials. We encourage suppliers to use minimal and recyclable packaging.
- **Store Operations:** We implement waste segregation and recycling programmes in all our stores. We aim to send zero waste to landfill by 2030.
- **Product Lifecycle:** We offer repair and recycling services for old equipment and clothing, helping extend the life cycle of our products.

### Water Conservation

We are committed to conserving water within our operations:

- **Efficient Systems:** We install water-saving fixtures in our stores and distribution centres.
- **Employee Awareness:** We educate our staff on water conservation practices and encourage them to adopt water-saving habits.

### Community Engagement

We believe in leading by example and engaging our community in sustainability efforts:

- **Customer Education:** We inform our customers about the importance of sustainability and how they can make eco-friendly choices.
- **Partnerships:** We collaborate with local environmental groups and organisations to support conservation projects and initiatives.

### Continuous Improvement

We recognise that sustainability is an ongoing journey. We are committed to continuously reviewing and improving our environmental practices, measuring our impact, and setting new targets for future progress.

At Meridian Outdoor Co., our environmental commitment is woven into the fabric of our business. We invite our customers, suppliers, and staff to join us in this vital effort to protect our planet.
