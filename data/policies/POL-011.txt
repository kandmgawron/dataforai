## Down Product Care Instructions

### Introduction

Welcome to Meridian Outdoor Co., your trusted destination for premium outdoor equipment and clothing across Scotland and Northern England. We take pride in offering high-quality products, including a range of down garments and gear. To ensure the longevity and performance of your down products, proper care and maintenance are essential. Follow these guidelines to keep your down items in top condition.

### General Care Tips

**1. Storage**

- **Cool and Dry Place:** Store your down products in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Humidity can cause mildew growth, which can damage down feathers.
- **Avoid Compression:** Do not compress down items for extended periods, as this can cause the down clusters to lose their loft and insulating properties. Use a breathable mesh bag for storage.

**2. Cleaning**

- **Spot Cleaning:** For minor stains or dirt, use a mild soap and damp cloth to gently spot clean the affected area. Avoid soaking the garment or gear.
- **Professional Cleaning:** For more extensive cleaning, take your down product to a professional cleaner who specialises in down garments. They have the expertise and equipment to clean and restore your item without damaging the down fill.

### Washing Instructions

**1. Preparation**

- **Check Labels:** Always check the care label on your down product for specific washing instructions.
- **Zip and Fasten:** Ensure all zippers, buttons, and fastenings are secure before washing.

**2. Washing Process**

- **Use a Mild Detergent:** Use a mild, fragrance-free detergent suitable for down products. Avoid harsh chemicals and bleach.
- **Cold Water:** Wash in cold water to prevent the down feathers from clumping together.
- **Gentle Cycle:** Select the gentle cycle on your washing machine. Avoid using the spin cycle, as this can damage the down fill.

**3. Drying Instructions**

- **Air Dry:** Lay your down product flat on a clean, dry surface in a well-ventilated area. Avoid using a dryer, as the heat can destroy the down feathers.
- **Fluff Regularly:** Gently fluff and reshape the down item periodically to restore its loft and insulation properties.
- **Indirect Sunlight:** If possible, place the item in indirect sunlight to help naturally dry any remaining moisture.

### Repair and Maintenance

**1. Minor Repairs**

- **Seams and Stitches:** For minor seam or stitch repairs, use a strong, waterproof thread that matches the garment. Ensure stitches are neat and secure.
- **Waterproofing:** If your down product has a waterproof coating, reapply as necessary following the manufacturer's instructions.

**2. Professional Repairs**

- **Complex Issues:** For more complex issues or significant damage, consult a professional tailor or outdoor gear specialist. They can provide expert repairs to restore your product's functionality and appearance.

### Conclusion

By following these care instructions, you can ensure that your down products from Meridian Outdoor Co. remain in excellent condition for many adventures to come. Proper storage, cleaning, and maintenance will help preserve the quality and performance of your down gear, allowing you to enjoy the outdoors to the fullest.
