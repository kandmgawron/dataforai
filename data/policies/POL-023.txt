## FAQs: Product Care

### **Why is proper product care important?**

Proper care of your outdoor equipment and clothing not only extends its lifespan but also ensures your safety and comfort. Well-maintained gear performs reliably, keeping you protected in various weather conditions.

### **How should I wash my outdoor clothing?**

Follow these guidelines to maintain the quality and performance of your outdoor clothing:

- **Read the Label:** Always check the care label on your garment for specific washing instructions.
- **Use Gentle Detergents:** Use a mild, non-bio detergent. Avoid detergents with optical brighteners or fabric softeners, as these can damage waterproof and breathable membranes.
- **Wash Inside Out:** Turn your garments inside out to protect the outer surface and any coatings.
- **Wash Separately:** Wash new items separately from old ones to prevent the transfer of manufacturing residues.
- **Low Temperature:** Use a low temperature setting, preferably 30°C or below.
- **Gentle Cycle:** Select a gentle or delicate wash cycle to minimise wear and tear.
- **Air Dry:** Do not tumble dry. Hang your clothing in a well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight to air dry. This helps maintain the integrity of waterproof and breathable membranes.

### **How do I maintain waterproof and breathable gear?**

Maintaining the effectiveness of waterproof and breathable gear is crucial for your comfort and safety:

- **Regular Cleaning:** Clean your gear regularly according to the manufacturer’s instructions. For minor dirt, use a soft brush and clean water. For more stubborn stains, use a mild detergent as mentioned above.
- **Avoid Heat:** Do not expose your gear to high temperatures. Avoid using hair dryers, radiators, or tumble dryers, as heat can damage the waterproof membrane and coatings.
- **Repair Promptly:** If you notice any tears or holes, repair them promptly using appropriate patches or seam sealant. This prevents water from seeping into the material.
- **Repelency Renewal:** Over time, the DWR (Durably Water Repellent) finish can wear off. You can reapply a DWR spray following the product instructions to restore the water-repellent properties.

### **How should I store my outdoor gear?**

Proper storage helps maintain the condition of your gear:

- **Dry Storage:** Store your gear in a dry, well-ventilated area. Avoid storing damp gear, as moisture can lead to mould and mildew.
- **Avoid Compression:** Do not compress your gear for long periods, as this can damage waterproof membranes and padding. Use stuff sacks if available.
- **Organise Properly:** Keep items organised and avoid hanging heavy items on hangers, which can stretch fabric and cause damage.

### **What should I do if my gear gets damaged?**

If your gear gets damaged, follow these steps:

- **Identify the Issue:** Determine the nature of the damage. Small holes, tears, or worn-out seams are common issues.
- **Use Repairs Kits:** Many manufacturers provide repair kits. These kits usually contain patches and adhesive sealants. Follow the instructions carefully to ensure a proper repair.
