## Sale & Clearance Terms

### Introduction

Welcome to Meridian Outdoor Co., your trusted destination for premium outdoor equipment and clothing. These Sale & Clearance Terms outline the conditions that apply to items marked down as part of our seasonal sales, end-of-range promotions, and clearance events. By purchasing items on sale or clearance, you agree to these terms.

### Price Guarantees

All sale and clearance prices are final and cannot be combined with additional discounts, including loyalty points, vouchers, or other promotional offers unless explicitly stated. 

Prices are subject to change without notice, although we will not charge you more than the price stated on the product at the time of purchase.

### Sale Items

Items included in any sale are clearly marked with a discount percentage or price. Sale items may not be exchanged or refunded unless defective, and all sale items are subject to a 30-day return policy from the date of purchase, provided they are returned in their original condition with tags attached.

### Clearance Items

Clearance items are excess or seasonal stock and are heavily discounted to clear inventory. 

**Key points to note:**

- **No exchanges or refunds:** Clearance items are sold on a strict “no exchange, no refund” basis unless the product is defective. 
- **Final sale:** Purchases are considered final, and we cannot accept returns for clearance items beyond the 30-day window, except in the case of defects. 
- **Limited stock:** Clearance items are available while stocks last, and we cannot hold items aside for future purchase.

### Returns and Refunds

All non-clearance sale items are eligible for an exchange or refund within 30 days of purchase, provided they are unused, in their original packaging, and with security tags attached. 

For defective sale or clearance items, please refer to our **Warranty and Faulty Goods Policy**.

### Online vs. In-Store Differences

Sale and clearance items are available both in-store and online, but stock levels may vary. Online orders for clearance items are subject to the same “no exchange, no refund” policy as in-store purchases.

### Pricing Errors

If a pricing error is discovered after you have purchased an item, Meridian Outdoor Co. will contact you to discuss the appropriate action. We will either refund the difference if the item was purchased at an incorrect price or, where applicable, complete the sale at the correct lower price where the error represents a reduction.

If you have any questions about these Sale & Clearance Terms, please visit your nearest Meridian Outdoor Co. store or contact our customer service team via phone or email. Our stores are located across Scotland and Northern England. Details of our locations can be found on our website.
