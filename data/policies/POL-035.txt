**Accessibility Statement for Meridian Outdoor Co.**

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we are committed to ensuring that our services and facilities are accessible to all our customers, including those with disabilities. We aim to provide an inclusive shopping experience for everyone, whether visiting our stores or accessing our online platforms.

**Our Commitment**

Meridian Outdoor Co. is dedicated to complying with the Equality Act 2010 and ensuring an environment that is welcoming and accessible to all. We continuously strive to enhance accessibility and meet the needs of our diverse customer base.

**Accessibility in Our Stores**

Our 12 stores located across Scotland and Northern England have been designed to be accessible. Key features include:

- **Wheelchair Access:** All our stores are fully wheelchair accessible, with ramps and wide aisles to facilitate easy movement.
- **Assisted Shopping Services:** We offer assisted shopping services for customers who need additional support. Staff are trained to assist with navigation, product selection, and checkout processes.
- **Accessible Changing Rooms:** We are progressively upgrading our changing rooms to include accessible facilities. Customers can enquire about availability at their nearest store.
- **Clear Signage:** Our stores feature clear signage to help customers navigate easily, including directional signs to key areas such as the checkout, restrooms, and customer service desks.

**Online Accessibility**

Our website and online services are designed to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) Level AA standards. We are committed to making our digital platforms accessible to all users, including those who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers. Features include:

- **Readable Content:** We use clear and simple language, appropriate font sizes, and high-contrast modes to enhance readability.
- **Keyboard Navigation:** Our website can be fully navigated using a keyboard, ensuring access for customers who cannot use a mouse.
- **Alt Text for Images:** All images on our website include alt text to describe the content for screen readers.

**Feedback and Contact**

We actively welcome feedback to improve our accessibility further. If you experience any difficulties accessing our services or have suggestions for improvement, please contact our Customer Service team at **customerservice@meridianoutdoors.co.uk** or by phone at **0800 123 4567**.
