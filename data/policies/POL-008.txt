### Determining Your Backpack Size

Backpack sizes are typically measured in litres and can range from small day packs to large expedition sacks. Here’s how to determine the right size for your activities:

**Day Hikes & Short Trips (20-30L)**

- **Ideal for:** Short hikes, day trips, and urban use.
- **Features:** Compact design, enough space for essentials like water, snacks, a map, and layers.
- **Best For:** Lightweight adventures where you need to carry only the basics.

**Multi-Day Hikes & Weekend Trips (40-60L)**

- **Ideal for:** Multi-day hikes, weekend trips, and photography excursions.
- **Features:** Ample space for sleeping bags, clothing, food, water, and other gear. Adjustable suspension systems for better load distribution.
- **Best For:** Those who need to carry more gear for extended trips.

**Long-Distance Trekking & Expedition (60L+)**

- **Ideal for:** Long-distance trekking, mountaineering, and expedition trips.
- **Features:** Large capacity, robust construction, and advanced suspension systems. Often includes external attachment points for extra gear.
- **Best For:** Serious adventurers who need to carry heavy loads over long distances.

### Ensuring Proper Fit

A well-fitted backpack is crucial for comfort and performance. Follow these steps to ensure a proper fit:

**Measure Your Torso**

- **Method:** Measure from the base of your neck (C7 vertebra) to the top of your hip (iliac crest).
- **Use:** This measurement helps you determine the correct torso length of your backpack. Most backpacks come with adjustable torso lengths to accommodate different body types.

**Adjust the Straps**

- **Shoulder Straps:** Adjust so they are snug but comfortable, evenly distributing the weight between both shoulders.
- **Hip Belts:** Tighten the hip belt to transfer most of the backpack’s weight to your hips, reducing strain on your shoulders.
- **Chest Strap:** Use the chest strap to stabilise the load and prevent the backpack from swaying side to side.

**Test the Fit**

- **In-Store:** Try on backpacks with and without weight to get a feel for their fit and comfort.
- **On-Trail:** If possible, take a short walk to test the backpack’s fit and adjust as necessary.

### Additional Tips

- **Break In Your Backpack:** New backpacks may feel stiff initially. Use them for short trips to break them in before longer adventures.
- **Organise Your Gear:** Proper organisation helps distribute weight evenly and prevents items from shifting during your hike.
