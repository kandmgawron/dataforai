## FAQs: Orders & Delivery

### How can I place an order?

You can place an order with Meridian Outdoor Co. either online through our website or in person at any of our 12 stores across Scotland and Northern England. Our online platform offers a convenient shopping experience, allowing you to browse our full range of outdoor equipment and clothing at any time.

### What are your delivery options?

We offer a variety of delivery options to suit your needs:

- **Standard Delivery**: Free on orders over £50. Orders are typically dispatched within 1-2 business days and delivered within 3-5 working days.
- **Next Day Delivery**: Available for an additional fee. Orders must be placed before 3pm Monday to Friday to be delivered the next business day.
- **Click & Collect**: Collect your order from your nearest Meridian Outdoor Co. store. Orders are usually ready within 2-4 hours, or you can schedule a later pickup time.

### Do you ship internationally?

At this time, Meridian Outdoor Co. only ships within the UK. We are committed to serving our local customer base across Scotland and Northern England.

### Can I track my order?

Yes, once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number to track your delivery on our website or through the courier's tracking system.

### What is your return policy?

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you need to return an item, you can do so within 100 days of purchase for a refund, exchange, or credit. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and with any tags attached. 

**Returns Process:**
- **Online Orders**: Return items to your nearest Meridian Outdoor Co. store, or we can arrange a return collection for an additional fee.
- **In-Store Purchases**: Returns can be processed directly at the store where you made the purchase.

### Can I change or cancel my order?

You can cancel or modify your order within 24 hours of placement. After this period, changes may not be possible, depending on the status of your order. Please contact customer service as soon as possible to discuss your options.

### How do I contact customer service regarding my order?

Our customer service team is here to help. You can reach us via:

- **Phone**: 0800 123 4567, available Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm.
- **Email**: customerservice@meridianoutdoor.co.uk
- **In-Store**: Visit any of our locations across Scotland and Northern England.

### Are there any additional charges I should be aware of?

For standard delivery, there are no additional charges on orders over £50. Next day delivery and return collections incur additional fees, which will be clearly stated before you complete your order. We aim to be transparent with all our pricing.

### Do you offer any loyalty or rewards programmes that affect delivery?

Our Meridian Rewards programme offers various benefits, but these do not affect delivery options or costs at this time. However, members often enjoy exclusive offers and early access to sales, which can help reduce the total cost of your orders.
