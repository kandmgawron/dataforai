## Standard UK Delivery Policy

### Overview

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we are committed to providing our customers with reliable, efficient, and transparent delivery services across the UK. Our standard UK delivery policy ensures that your outdoor equipment and clothing reach you in a timely and secure manner, allowing you to enjoy the great outdoors without delay.

### Delivery Areas

Our standard UK delivery service covers all areas within Scotland and Northern England, including all of our 12 physical store locations. For customers residing outside these regions, please contact our customer service team for customised delivery options and potential additional charges.

### Delivery Options

We offer the following standard delivery options to cater to your specific needs:

#### 1. Standard Delivery

- **Delivery Time:** 3-5 business days from the date of dispatch.
- **Cost:** Free on orders over £50. For orders below £50, a flat delivery fee of £3.95 applies.
- **Service:** This option uses our trusted courier partners to ensure your items are delivered safely and efficiently.

#### 2. Next Day Delivery

- **Delivery Time:** By the next business day if the order is placed before 3 PM.
- **Cost:** A fixed fee of £5.95.
- **Service:** Ideal for customers needing their items urgently. Orders must be placed before the cut-off time to qualify for next-day delivery.

### Order Processing

#### Processing Time

- **In-Store Purchases:** Orders are processed immediately or within 24 hours.
- **Online Orders:** Orders are processed within 1-2 business days, excluding weekends and bank holidays.

#### Dispatch

- **Method:** We use reputable courier services to ensure timely and secure delivery.
- **Tracking:** A tracking number will be provided via email once your order has been dispatched. You can use this number to monitor the delivery status of your items.

### Delivery Attempts

- **First Attempt:** Our couriers will attempt to deliver your order on the estimated delivery date.
- **Subsequent Attempts:** If no one is available to sign for the parcel, the courier will leave a calling card with instructions on how to rearrange delivery or collect from a local depot.
- **Failed Deliveries:** After three failed delivery attempts, the parcel will be returned to us. You will need to reorder and pay for shipping again.

### Delivery Charges

- **Free Delivery:** Orders over £50 qualify for free standard delivery.
- **Additional Charges:** Additional charges may apply for remote areas or islands. Customers will be informed of any extra costs before finalising the order.
