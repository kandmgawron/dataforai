## Ethical Sourcing & Supply Chain Policy

### Introduction

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of ethical sourcing and supply chain management. As a UK-based outdoor equipment and clothing retailer with 12 stores across Scotland and Northern England, we understand the importance of responsible practices that protect both people and the planet. This policy outlines our approach to ensuring ethical practices throughout our supply chain.

### Our Ethical Principles

Our ethical sourcing framework is built on the following core principles:

- **Fair Labour Practices:** We are committed to ensuring fair wages, reasonable working hours, and safe working conditions for all workers in our supply chain. We prohibit child labour, forced labour, and any form of discrimination. We aim to foster an environment of respect and equality.
- **Environmental Responsibility:** Sustainability is at the heart of our operations. We strive to minimise our environmental impact by sourcing materials responsibly, reducing waste, and promoting energy efficiency. We support the use of sustainable, recycled, and organic materials where possible.
- **Transparency:** We believe in transparency at every stage of our supply chain. We work closely with our suppliers to ensure they adhere to our ethical standards and are committed to sharing information about our sourcing practices with our customers.

### Supply Chain Management

To uphold our ethical principles, we have implemented the following measures:

**Supplier Code of Conduct:** All suppliers are required to adhere to our Supplier Code of Conduct, which outlines our expectations for ethical and sustainable practices. This code covers areas such as labour standards, environmental protection, and business ethics.

**Regular Audits:** We conduct regular audits of our suppliers to ensure compliance with our ethical standards. These audits are designed to identify any potential issues and provide support to suppliers to improve their practices.

**Capacity Building:** We invest in capacity-building initiatives to help our suppliers implement sustainable and ethical practices. This includes training on topics such as waste reduction, energy efficiency, and fair labour practices.

**Collaboration:** We collaborate with industry organisations and stakeholders to promote best practices in ethical sourcing and supply chain management. By working together, we can drive positive change across the entire industry.

### Customer Commitment

We are committed to informing our customers about our ethical sourcing and supply chain practices. Through clear labelling, product information, and customer engagement, we aim to raise awareness about the importance of ethical choices and empower our customers to make informed decisions.

### Conclusion

At Meridian Outdoor Co., ethical sourcing and supply chain management are not just policies—they are integral to our identity. We are dedicated to making a positive impact on people and the planet, and we will continue to strive for excellence in all that we do.
