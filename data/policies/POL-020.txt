## International Shipping Policy for Meridian Outdoor Co.

At Meridian Outdoor Co., we strive to offer our high-quality outdoor equipment and clothing to customers worldwide. We understand that international shipping can seem complex, so we've created this policy to provide clear, detailed information about our procedures, costs, and delivery times.

### Shipping Destinations

We ship to over 200 countries and territories globally. Whether you're in the United States, Canada, Australia, or anywhere in between, you can enjoy the same great products that are popular in our stores across Scotland and Northern England.

### Shipping Methods and Delivery Times

We partner with trusted international courier services to ensure your order arrives safely and efficiently. The available shipping methods and estimated delivery times are as follows:

**Standard International Shipping**

- **Delivery Time:** 5–12 business days, depending on the destination country.
- **Cost:** Calculated at checkout based on the total weight and value of your order, as well as the destination country.
- **Tracking:** All standard international shipments include tracking information, which will be provided in your confirmation email.

**Express International Shipping**

- **Delivery Time:** 2–5 business days, depending on the destination country.
- **Cost:** Higher than standard shipping, due to faster transit times. The exact cost will be displayed at checkout.
- **Tracking:** Includes full tracking and priority handling to ensure your order arrives as quickly as possible.

**Note:** Delivery times may vary due to customs processing, local holidays, or other unforeseen circumstances. We recommend allowing extra time for these potential delays, especially during peak seasons.

### Customs and Import Duties

Our customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import charges that may apply to their order. These fees are not included in the product price or shipping cost and will be payable upon delivery or collection of your order, depending on the destination country's customs procedures.

We advise customers to check their country's customs regulations before placing an order, as failure to pay any applicable duties or taxes may result in your package being returned to us or disposed of by customs authorities.

### Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated based on the following factors:

- **Destination Country:** Shipping costs vary depending on the country to which you are shipping.
- **Order Value:** Larger or more valuable orders may incur higher shipping fees.
- **Shipping Method:** As noted above, express shipping will cost more than standard shipping.

The exact shipping cost for your order will be displayed during the checkout process, allowing you to make informed decisions before completing your purchase.

### Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to track your shipment. We recommend bookmarking this link or saving the tracking number for future reference. If you have any issues with tracking your order, please contact our customer service team for assistance.

### Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about international shipping, please don't hesitate to contact our customer service team. We are here to help ensure your shopping experience with Meridian Outdoor Co. is seamless and satisfying.
